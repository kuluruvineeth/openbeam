# Zendesk Quick Start

## Prerequisites

- Zendesk account with Admin access
- Your Zendesk subdomain (e.g., `yourcompany` from `yourcompany.zendesk.com`)
- OpenBeam instance with HTTPS endpoint

## 1. Create an OAuth Client

1. Log in to your Zendesk Admin Center at `https://yourcompany.zendesk.com/admin`
2. Navigate to **Apps and integrations > APIs > Zendesk API**
3. Click the **OAuth Clients** tab
4. Click **Add OAuth Client**
5. Fill in:
   - **Client Name**: OpenBeam
   - **Description**: OpenBeam enterprise search integration
   - **Redirect URLs**:
     ```text
     https://your-domain.com/connectors/setup/zendesk/oauth/callback
     ```
6. Click **Save**

## 2. Get Credentials

1. After saving, your **Client ID** is displayed (also called the Unique Identifier)
2. Copy the **Secret** shown once after creation

<Callout type="warn">
The client secret is only displayed once after creation. Copy it immediately and store it securely.
</Callout>

## 3. Configure Environment

```bash
ZENDESK_CLIENT_ID=your-client-id
ZENDESK_CLIENT_SECRET=your-client-secret
ZENDESK_SUBDOMAIN=yourcompany
WEB_URL=https://your-domain.com
SERVER_URL=https://api.your-domain.com
```

## 4. Connect in OpenBeam

1. Go to **Settings > Connectors > Add Connector > Zendesk**
2. Enter your **subdomain**, **Client ID**, and **Client Secret**
3. Click **Connect** and authorize in Zendesk
4. Initial sync starts automatically

The initial sync indexes all tickets and optionally help center articles and comments. Large instances with hundreds of thousands of tickets may take significant time.
