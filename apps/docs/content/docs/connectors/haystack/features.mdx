# Features

## Indexed Entities

### People
Full-text search across employee names, titles, departments, teams, bios, and contact information. Metadata includes email, phone, manager, location, start date, and pronouns.

### Teams
Search across team names, descriptions, and member lists. Includes lead assignment, member count, and individual member names.

### Departments
Department hierarchy with names, descriptions, headcount, and leadership. Includes parent department relationships for navigating the org structure.

### Locations
Office location directory with names, addresses, cities, countries, timezones, and capacity. Enables quick lookup of office details.

## Actions

The connector supports write operations for agent workflows:

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Update Person** | Update a person's title, phone, bio, or pronouns |

## Knowledge Graph

The connector feeds the knowledge graph with:
- **Manager relationships** from employee profile hierarchy
- **Team membership** linking people to their teams
- **Department structure** with parent-child relationships
- **Location assignment** connecting people to offices
