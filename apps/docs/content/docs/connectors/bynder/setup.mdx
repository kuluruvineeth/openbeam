# Bynder Setup

## Prerequisites

- A Bynder account with admin access
- Access to Bynder portal settings (OAuth Apps)

## Step 1: Register an OAuth Application

1. Log into your Bynder portal (e.g., `https://acme.bynder.com`)
2. Go to **Settings > OAuth Apps**
3. Click **Add new app**
4. Name it "OpenBeam Integration"
5. Set the redirect URI to your OpenBeam callback URL
6. Grant scopes: `offline`, `asset:read`, `collection:read`
7. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**

## Step 2: Determine Your Domain

Your Bynder domain is the subdomain in your portal URL. For example, if your URL is `https://acme.bynder.com`, your domain is `acme`.

## Step 3: Add Connector in OpenBeam

1. Navigate to **Settings > Connectors**
2. Click **Add Connector** and select **Bynder**
3. Enter your **Domain** (e.g., `acme`)
4. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**
5. Click **Connect** to start the OAuth flow
6. Authorize OpenBeam in the Bynder consent screen

## Step 4: Initial Sync

After authorization, OpenBeam runs a full sync of your Bynder data. The first sync indexes all assets, collections, and tags from your portal.
