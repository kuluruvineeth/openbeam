# Troubleshooting

## Authentication Errors

### "Authentication failed" during OAuth
- Verify your Client ID and Client Secret are correct
- Ensure the redirect URL in your Harvest OAuth application matches exactly
- Check that your Harvest account is active and not suspended

### Token refresh failures
- Harvest tokens expire after 18 hours (64,800 seconds)
- If refresh fails, reconnect the connector via OAuth
- Ensure the OAuth application has not been revoked in Harvest

## Sync Issues

### Missing time entries
- The `updated_since` filter only returns records modified after the timestamp
- Running timers update continuously and may appear in every sync
- Check if the time entries are in a different Harvest account

### Rate limit errors
- Harvest allows 100 requests per 15 seconds
- Large accounts with many time entries may trigger rate limits during full sync
- The connector automatically retries with backoff

### Invoices not syncing
- Verify "Sync Invoices" is enabled in connector settings
- Draft invoices are included in the sync
- Deleted invoices will not appear in results

## Account Selection

### Wrong account connected
- Harvest OAuth returns all accounts the user has access to
- The connector uses the first account in the list
- To connect a different account, disconnect and reconnect with the desired account
- Ensure you are logged into the correct Harvest account before authorizing

## Performance

### Slow initial sync
- Full sync time depends on the number of time entries and invoices
- Consider setting a lookback period to limit the initial sync scope
- Subsequent incremental syncs are significantly faster
