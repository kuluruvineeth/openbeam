# Evernote Setup

## Prerequisites

- An Evernote account (Basic, Premium, or Business)
- Access to the Evernote developer portal

## Step 1: Generate a Developer Token

1. Visit [dev.evernote.com](https://dev.evernote.com/get-api-key)
2. Sign in with your Evernote account
3. Navigate to the developer token section
4. Click **Create a developer token**
5. Copy the generated token

For testing, you can use the sandbox environment at sandbox.evernote.com.

## Step 2: Add Connector in OpenBeam

1. Navigate to **Settings > Connectors**
2. Click **Add Connector** and select **Evernote**
3. Enter your **Developer Token**
4. Select the **Environment** (Production or Sandbox)
5. Click **Connect** to validate and activate

## Step 3: Initial Sync

After activation, OpenBeam runs a full sync of your Evernote data. The first sync fetches all notebooks, then all notes with their content, and optionally all tags.
