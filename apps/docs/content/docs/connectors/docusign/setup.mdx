# DocuSign Setup

## Prerequisites

- A DocuSign account (demo or production)
- Admin access to create apps in DocuSign Settings

## Step 1: Create a DocuSign App

1. Log into [DocuSign](https://account.docusign.com)
2. Go to **Settings > Apps and Keys**
3. Click **Add App and Integration Key**
4. Name it something like "OpenBeam Integration"
5. Copy the **Integration Key** (this is your Client ID)

## Step 2: Configure the App

1. Under **Authentication**, add a **Secret Key** and copy it
2. Under **Additional Settings > Redirect URIs**, add your OpenBeam callback URL:
   - Production: `https://your-domain.com/connectors/setup/docusign/oauth/callback`
   - Development: `http://localhost:3001/connectors/setup/docusign/oauth/callback`
3. Save the app configuration

## Step 3: Add Connector in OpenBeam

1. Navigate to **Settings > Connectors**
2. Click **Add Connector** and select **DocuSign**
3. Enter your **Integration Key** (Client ID)
4. Enter your **Secret Key** (Client Secret)
5. Select your **Environment**:
   - **Production** for live DocuSign accounts
   - **Demo** for sandbox/testing accounts
6. Click **Connect** to start the OAuth flow
7. Authorize OpenBeam in the DocuSign consent screen

## Step 4: Initial Sync

After authorization, OpenBeam runs a full sync of your DocuSign data. The first sync fetches envelopes from the configured lookback period (default: 365 days), plus all templates and folders.
