# Showpad Setup

## Prerequisites

- A Showpad account with admin access
- Access to Showpad Admin portal for OAuth app registration

## Step 1: Determine Your Subdomain

Your Showpad subdomain is the part before `.showpad.biz` in your Showpad URL. For example, if your URL is `https://acme.showpad.biz`, your subdomain is `acme`.

## Step 2: Register an OAuth App

1. Log into the [Showpad Admin](https://admin.showpad.biz)
2. Navigate to **Integrations > OAuth Apps**
3. Click **Add OAuth App**
4. Enter:
   - **App Name:** OpenBeam Integration
   - **Redirect URI:** `https://your-openbeam-domain.com/connectors/setup/showpad/oauth/callback`
5. Copy the generated **Client ID** and **Client Secret**

## Step 3: Add Connector in OpenBeam

1. Navigate to **Settings > Connectors**
2. Click **Add Connector** and select **Showpad**
3. Enter your **Subdomain** (e.g., `acme`)
4. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**
5. Click **Connect** to start the OAuth flow
6. Authorize access in the Showpad consent screen

## Step 4: Initial Sync

After authorization, OpenBeam runs a full sync of your Showpad content. The first sync indexes all assets, channels, experiences, and tags.
