# Lessonly Setup

## Prerequisites

- A Lessonly (Seismic Learning) account with admin access
- Access to Lessonly API settings

## Step 1: Generate an API Key

1. Log into your Lessonly account at `https://yourcompany.lessonly.com`
2. Go to **Settings > Integrations**
3. Locate the **API** section
4. Click **Generate API Key** or copy your existing key
5. Note your **subdomain** (the part before `.lessonly.com`)

## Step 2: Add Connector in OpenBeam

1. Navigate to **Settings > Connectors**
2. Click **Add Connector** and select **Lessonly**
3. Enter your **API Key**
4. Enter your **Subdomain** (e.g., `mycompany`)
5. Click **Connect** to validate and activate

## Step 3: Initial Sync

After activation, OpenBeam runs a full sync of your Lessonly data. The first sync fetches all lessons, learning paths, assignments, groups, and users. This may take several minutes depending on your content volume.
