# Setup

## Prerequisites

- An Intercom workspace with admin access
- Access to the Intercom Developer Hub

## Step 1: Create an Intercom App

1. Go to the [Intercom Developer Hub](https://developers.intercom.com/)
2. Click **New app** and give it a name (e.g., "OpenBeam Search")
3. Select your workspace

## Step 2: Configure OAuth

1. In your app settings, go to **Authentication**
2. Enable **Use OAuth**
3. Add the redirect URL: `https://your-openbeam-domain.com/connectors/setup/intercom/oauth/callback`
4. Note your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**

## Step 3: Connect in OpenBeam

1. Go to **Settings > Connectors** in OpenBeam
2. Click **Add Connector** and select Intercom
3. Enter your Client ID and Client Secret
4. Click **Connect** to start the OAuth flow
5. Authorize the app in Intercom
6. The connector will activate and begin syncing

## Permissions

Intercom OAuth scopes are implied by the OAuth flow. The app will have access to:

- Read conversations
- Read articles and collections
- Read contacts
- Read admins and teams
