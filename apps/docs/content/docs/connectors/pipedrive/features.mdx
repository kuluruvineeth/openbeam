# Features

## Indexed Entities

### Deals
Full-text search across deal titles, values, stages, and pipeline information. Metadata includes value, currency, stage, pipeline, expected close date, and probability.

### Persons (Contacts)
Search across contact names, email addresses, and phone numbers. Organization associations are preserved for context.

### Organizations
Company records with name, address, and people count indexed for search.

### Activities
Tasks, calls, meetings, and other activities with subject lines, types, due dates, and notes.

### Notes
Free-text notes attached to deals, persons, or organizations. HTML content is stripped for clean indexing.

## Knowledge Graph

The connector feeds the knowledge graph with:
- **Author attribution** from deal and record owners
- **Organization relationships** between persons and companies
- **Activity associations** linking activities to deals and contacts
