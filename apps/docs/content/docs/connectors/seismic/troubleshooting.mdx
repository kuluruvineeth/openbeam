# Seismic Troubleshooting

## Authentication Errors

### "Token exchange failed"
- Verify your Client ID and Client Secret are correct
- Ensure the redirect URI in your Seismic OAuth app matches exactly
- Check that the OAuth app has the required scopes

### "Token refresh failed"
- The refresh token may have expired. Re-authenticate by disconnecting and reconnecting the connector
- Verify the OAuth app has not been deleted or revoked in Seismic

## Sync Issues

### No content appearing after sync
- Verify your Seismic account has content in at least one workspace
- Check that the OAuth app has the `seismic.library.view` scope
- Review the connector logs for API errors

### Slow sync performance
- Large content libraries may take time on the initial full sync
- Subsequent incremental syncs will be faster
- Consider setting a lookback period to limit initial sync scope

### Rate limit errors
- The connector automatically handles rate limits with retry logic
- If persistent, reduce the sync frequency in connector settings

## Connection Issues

### "Connector configuration not found"
- Re-enter your Client ID and Client Secret in the connector settings
- Ensure the connector was properly created before attempting OAuth
