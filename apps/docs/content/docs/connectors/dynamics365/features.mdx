# Features

## Indexed Entities

### Accounts
Full-text search across company names, industries, revenue figures, and address details. Metadata includes annual revenue, employee count, phone numbers, and ownership information.

### Contacts
Search across contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, and job titles. Account associations are preserved so you can find contacts by their parent company.

### Leads
Prospective customer records with name, email, company name, topic of interest, and lead rating. Useful for searching across the top of your sales funnel.

### Opportunities
Sales opportunity records with deal name, estimated value, sales stage, expected close date, and win probability. Enables searching across your active pipeline.

### Cases (Incidents)
Customer service cases with title, priority, status, and description. Linked to customer accounts and contacts for full context during support searches.

### Activities
Tasks, appointments, emails, and phone calls with subject lines, activity type, status, due dates, and descriptions. Covers all tracked interactions in your CRM.

## Actions

The connector supports write operations through the Dataverse Web API:

- **Create and update accounts** — programmatically manage company records
- **Create and update contacts** — add or modify people in your CRM
- **Create and update opportunities** — manage deals from agents or canvas workflows

## Knowledge Graph

The connector feeds the knowledge graph with:
- **Owner attribution** from record owners and created-by users
- **Account relationships** between contacts, leads, and their parent accounts
- **Opportunity associations** linking deals to accounts and contacts
- **Case associations** connecting service incidents to customers
