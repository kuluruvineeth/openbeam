# Procore Setup

## Prerequisites

- A Procore account with admin access
- Access to the Procore Developer Portal

## Step 1: Create a Procore Developer App

1. Go to [developers.procore.com](https://developers.procore.com/)
2. Sign in with your Procore credentials
3. Navigate to **My Apps** and click **Create New App**
4. Choose **Data Connection** as the app type
5. Fill in the app details (name, description)
6. Under **OAuth Redirect URI**, add your OpenBeam callback URL:
   `https://your-openbeam-domain.com/connectors/setup/procore/oauth/callback`
7. Note the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**

## Step 2: Add Connector in OpenBeam

1. Navigate to **Settings > Connectors**
2. Click **Add Connector** and select **Procore**
3. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**
4. Click **Connect** to start the OAuth flow
5. Authorize OpenBeam in the Procore login screen

## Step 3: Initial Sync

After authorization, OpenBeam runs a full sync of your Procore data. The first sync fetches all active projects and their associated RFIs, submittals, documents, and drawings.

## Procore-Company-Id Header

Procore's API requires a `Procore-Company-Id` header on all requests. OpenBeam automatically detects your company during OAuth and includes this header on every API call.
