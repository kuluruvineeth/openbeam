# Egnyte Setup

## Prerequisites

- An Egnyte account with admin access
- Access to the Egnyte Developer Portal

## Step 1: Create an API Application

1. Go to the [Egnyte Developer Portal](https://developers.egnyte.com/)
2. Sign in with your Egnyte admin account
3. Navigate to **My Apps** and click **Create New Application**
4. Fill in the application details:
   - **Application Name**: OpenBeam Integration
   - **Application Type**: Internal Application
5. Note the **Client ID** (API Key) and **Client Secret**
6. Add the OAuth redirect URI provided by OpenBeam

## Step 2: Identify Your Domain

Your Egnyte domain is the subdomain of your Egnyte URL. For example, if your URL is `https://acme.egnyte.com`, your domain is `acme`.

## Step 3: Add Connector in OpenBeam

1. Navigate to **Settings > Connectors**
2. Click **Add Connector** and select **Egnyte**
3. Enter your **Domain** (e.g., `acme`)
4. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**
5. Click **Connect** to begin the OAuth flow
6. Authorize OpenBeam in the Egnyte consent screen

## Step 4: Initial Sync

After authorization, OpenBeam runs a full sync of your Egnyte content. The first sync recursively traverses all folders starting from the root folder path (default: `/Shared`), indexing files, folders, and shared links.
