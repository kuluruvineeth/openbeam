# Jenkins Setup

## Prerequisites

- A Jenkins instance (2.x or later) accessible via HTTPS
- A Jenkins user account with read access to jobs, builds, and nodes

## Step 1: Generate an API Token

1. Log into your Jenkins instance
2. Click your username in the top-right corner
3. Go to **Configure**
4. Scroll to **API Token** section
5. Click **Add new Token**, name it "OpenBeam Integration"
6. Click **Generate** and copy the token immediately (it cannot be viewed again)

## Step 2: Verify Your URL

Your Jenkins instance URL should be the base URL without any trailing path:
- Correct: `https://jenkins.example.com`
- Correct: `https://ci.company.com:8443`
- Incorrect: `https://jenkins.example.com/job/my-project`

## Step 3: Add Connector in OpenBeam

1. Navigate to **Settings > Connectors**
2. Click **Add Connector** and select **Jenkins**
3. Enter your **Jenkins URL**
4. Enter your **Username** and **API Token**
5. Click **Connect** to validate and activate

## Step 4: Initial Sync

After activation, OpenBeam runs a full sync of your Jenkins instance. The first sync fetches all jobs, their recent builds (up to 25 per job by default), views, and agent nodes.
